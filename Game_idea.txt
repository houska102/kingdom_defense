- game about defending a kingdom 1 city at a time
- the city gets gradually destroyed in turns depeding on how well yure doing at the defence.\
    - there is no way to stall the destruction indefinitelly
- while defending a city, make descisions that will let you defend the next one better.
- 3 descicions at the start of the game.


Game loop:

1. make initial 3 decisions
2. depeding on the descicions a few new city tiles and defenders get added to your city building choices.
    - there are some default buildings and garrison to be placed as well
3. start a city
    - layout the city tiles and choose your garrison
    - later cities might have some prebuild tiles
    - there is a tile limit set by the grid of the city
4. city loop:
    1. multiple attacks get initiaded to different tiles
        - when there is less tiles than attacks some tiles will get much stronger attacks to indicate this
    2. player chooses what garrison to send to what attack
    4. this will get the final attack and defense values:
        - attack: Enemy attack with possible reductions from whatever tile and garrison passives
        - defense: Tile defense + Garrison strength
            - however much garrison strength gets used to defend the attack gets deducted first from garrison endurance and then its health
                - example with tile (defense 2, health 8) and garrison (strength 3, health 2, endurance 2)
                    - attack strength 2 - gets cancelled out by tile defense alone
                    - attack strength 4 - 2 gets canceled by defense, 2 by garrison strength so remove 2 endurance
                    - attack strength 5 - 2 gets canceled by defense, 3 by garrison strength so remove 2 endurance and 1 health
                    - attack strength 10 - 2 gets canceled by defense, 3 by garrison strength so remove 2 endurance and 1 health remaining 5 gets removed from tile health
                - if there are more than 1 garisons in assigned to the tile the endurance and health gets substracted by their attack ratio
    5. Make a choice
   ----- repat
---- repeat until all cities are destroyed
-get pernament point to spend


Decisions made (first prototype - single city):

- city grid is 4x4 (max 16 tiles)
- default buildings: Keep, Wall x3, House x4, Watchtower x2, Barracks
- default garrison: choose 3 of 5 (Militia, Spearmen, Knights, Archers, Veterans)
    - each unit has strength, health, endurance and maybe a passive
- combat order: passives reduce attack -> tile defense -> garrison strength -> tile health
    - multiple garrisons on one attack split the absorbed damage by strength ratio (rounded to whole numbers)
- attacks per turn: 2 + turn/2 (rounded down)
- attack strength grows every turn (~3 + turn*1.5 with random spread) so destruction can't be stalled forever
- attacks target living tiles from the enemy side (see attack direction below), extra attacks merge onto already attacked tiles (marked as stronger)
- garrison that rested this turn gets full endurance back, garrison that fought gets 1 back
- garrison at 0 health dies, tile at 0 health is destroyed
- city falls when the Keep is destroyed or no tiles are left
- "Make a choice" = pick 1 of 3 cards each turn
    - now cards: repair tile, rest garrison, recruit garrison, fortify tile (+1 defense this turn)
    - next city cards: unlock a new tile or garrison type for the next city
- score for now = turns survived

Attack direction:

- every city has 1 enemy side (north / east / south / west), rolled at random and shown while building the city
    - stays the same for the whole city
- lane = column (north/south) or row (east/west)
- rank = how many standing tiles are between the tile and the enemy edge in its lane
    - rank 0 = front line, marked red on the enemy side of the tile
    - empty cells and destroyed tiles dont count, gaps dont protect anything
- attacks hit the front line first, if there are more attacks than front line tiles the rest go to rank 1, then rank 2...
    - when front tiles get destroyed the next ones become the front line, so the city gets eaten from that side
- merging only happens when there are more attacks than standing tiles in the whole city

Events:

- 20% chance per attack to trigger an event, rolled after pressing End turn (player cant react to them)
    - only attacks with at least 1 assigned defender can get an event
- 70% of events are good, 30% bad
- events pop up as a modal one by one with some spice text and before -> after numbers, then the turn resolves
- each event only affects its own attack
- bad events:
    - Siege Ladders - attack +3
    - Berserker Charge - attack x1.5 (rounded up)
    - Sappers - tile defense ignored
    - Exhausted Defenders - each defender -1 strength
- good events:
    - Boiling Oil - tile defense +2
    - Commander Slain - attack halved (rounded down)
    - Mud and Rain - attack -3
    - Rallying Cry - each defender +1 strength
