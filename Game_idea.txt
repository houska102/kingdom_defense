- game about defending a kingdom 1 city at a time
- the city gets gradually destroyed in turns depeding on how well yure doing at the defence.\
    - there is no way to stall the destruction indefinitelly
- while defending a city, make descisions that will let you defend the next one better.
- 3 descicions at the start of the game.


Game loop:

1. make initial 3 decisions
2. depeding on the descicions a few new city tiles and defenders get added to your city building choices.
    - there are some default buildings and garrison to be placed as well
3. start a city
    - layout the city tiles and choose your garrison
    - later cities might have some prebuild tiles
    - there is a tile limit set by the grid of the city
4. city loop:
    1. multiple attacks get initiaded to different tiles
        - when there is less tiles than attacks some tiles will get much stronger attacks to indicate this
    2. player chooses what garrison to send to what attack
    4. this will get the final attack and defense values:
        - attack: Enemy attack with possible reductions from whatever tile and garrison passives
        - defense: Tile defense + Garrison strength
            - however much garrison strength gets used to defend the attack gets deducted first from garrison endurance and then its health
                - example with tile (defense 2, health 8) and garrison (strength 3, health 2, endurance 2)
                    - attack strength 2 - gets cancelled out by tile defense alone
                    - attack strength 4 - 2 gets canceled by defense, 2 by garrison strength so remove 2 endurance
                    - attack strength 5 - 2 gets canceled by defense, 3 by garrison strength so remove 2 endurance and 1 health
                    - attack strength 10 - 2 gets canceled by defense, 3 by garrison strength so remove 2 endurance and 1 health remaining 5 gets removed from tile health
                - if there are more than 1 garisons in assigned to the tile the endurance and health gets substracted by their attack ratio
    5. Make a choice
   ----- repat
---- repeat until all cities are destroyed
-get pernament point to spend


Decisions made (first prototype - single city):

- city grid grows over the campaign: 4x4 in the first third (cities 1-4), 5x5 in the second (5-7), 6x6 in the last (8-10)
    - prebuilt tiles are placed by x/y on that city's grid
- default buildings: Keep, Wall x3, House x4, Watchtower x2, Barracks
- default garrison: choose 3 of 5 (Militia, Spearmen, Knights, Archers, Veterans)
    - plus Sellswords as a free extra pick (see unit passives), they dont count toward the pool size for picks
    - each unit has strength, health, endurance and maybe a passive
- combat order: passives reduce attack -> tile defense -> garrison strength -> tile health
    - multiple garrisons on one attack split the absorbed damage by strength ratio (rounded to whole numbers)
- attacks per turn: 2 + turn/2 (rounded down), never more than the grid width (4x4 = 4, 5x5 = 5, 6x6 = 6)
- attack strength grows every turn (~3 + turn*1.5 with random spread) so destruction can't be stalled forever
- attacks target living tiles from the enemy side (see attack direction below), extra attacks merge onto already attacked tiles (marked as stronger)
- garrison that rested this turn gets full endurance back, garrison that fought gets 1 back
- garrison at 0 health dies, tile at 0 health is destroyed
- city falls when the Keep is destroyed or no tiles are left
- "Make a choice" = pick 1 of 3 cards each turn
    - now cards: repair tile, rest garrison, recruit garrison, fortify tile (+1 defense this turn), tribute (+1-3 gold)
    - next city cards: unlock a new tile or garrison type for all later cities
- score for now = turns survived

Attack direction:

- every city has 1 enemy side, shown while building the city
    - for now always north (random north / east / south / west is supported, switched off for now)
    - stays the same for the whole city
- lane = column (north/south) or row (east/west)
- rank = how many standing tiles are between the tile and the enemy edge in its lane
    - rank 0 = front line, marked red on the enemy side of the tile
    - empty cells and destroyed tiles dont count, gaps dont protect anything
- open flanks: the enemy can walk through empty / destroyed cells connected to its edge
    - any tile next to such a cell is exposed from that side and counts as front line (rank 0)
    - the red marker shows on every exposed side of the tile
    - holes fully enclosed by standing tiles dont expose anything
- attacks hit the front line first, if there are more attacks than front line tiles the rest go to rank 1, then rank 2...
    - attacks on deeper ranks (rank 1+) are half strength (rounded up), marked with ½
        - NOTE: with attacks capped at the grid width this currently never happens, the front line always has enough tiles
    - when front tiles get destroyed the next ones become the front line, so the city gets eaten from that side
- merging only happens when there are more attacks than standing tiles in the whole city

Events:

- 20% chance per attack to trigger an event, rolled after pressing End turn (player cant react to them)
    - only attacks with at least 1 assigned defender can get an event
- 70% of events are good, 30% bad
- events pop up as a modal one by one with some spice text and before -> after numbers, then the turn resolves
- each event only affects its own attack
- bad events:
    - Siege Ladders - attack +3
    - Berserker Charge - attack x1.5 (rounded up)
    - Sappers - tile defense ignored
    - Exhausted Defenders - each defender -1 strength
- good events:
    - Boiling Oil - tile defense +2
    - Commander Slain - attack halved (rounded down)
    - Mud and Rain - attack -3
    - Rallying Cry - each defender +1 strength

Battle events:

- every city rolls 2-3 battle events when its setup starts, they last for the whole defense of that city
    - pop up one by one as a modal (like normal events) when setup starts, so the layout and garrison can be planned around them
    - afterwards they stay listed in the side panel for the rest of the city
    - each event is 50% good / 50% bad, never two events of the same kind (so +1 and -1 attack cant both roll)
    - one-time effects (gold, units, damage) are applied when pressing "Defend the city" and written to the battle log
- good:
    - Famine in the Enemy Camp - every attack -1
    - Master Masons - every tile +1 defense
    - Volunteers - +1 garrison pick
    - War Chest - +3 gold
    - Favorable Omens - per-attack events +15% good chance
    - Royal Envoy - +1 card in every card pick
    - Wandering Sellswords - +1 random unit (locked ones included)
- bad:
    - Veteran Horde - every attack +1
    - Rotten Timber - every tile -1 defense (never below 0)
    - Second Army - +1 attack every turn, on top of the grid width cap
    - Desertion - -1 garrison pick
    - Ill Omens - per-attack events -20% good chance
    - Camp Fever - every unit starts with -1 HP (never below 1)
    - Earthquake - 2 random tiles start with -2 HP (never below 1)

Campaign (10 cities):

- each city is played until it falls, then you march to the next one
    - campaign ends when city 10 falls
- cities: Ashford, Brightwater, Coldharbour, Dunmere, Eastmarch, Fallowmoor, Greyhaven, Highcrag, Ironvale, Kingsreach
- unlocks picked in a city apply to every later city
    - tile unlock: +1 copy of that tile in the palette, stacks if picked again
    - unit unlock: unit is added to the garrison pool, never offered twice
    - recruit cards can also draw unlocked units
- garrison picks = 60% of the garrison pool (rounded down), never lower than 3
    - pool 5 -> 3 picks, 7 -> 4, 9 -> 5
- gold also carries over (see Gold below), nothing else does, garrison and tiles are fresh every city
- the last city offers no next city cards
- difficulty scaling:
    - every city adds +0.5 attack strength (city 1 +0, city 10 +4.5)
    - from city 3 on some tiles are prebuilt (often damaged), they cant be removed and take grid space
- unlockable tiles: Bastion, Infirmary, Stockade, Ballista Tower, Militia Center, Chapel, Last Bastion, Mercenary Camp, Granary
    - a tile can have more than one passive
    - Chapel (0 def, 4 HP): while standing, events are 80% good instead of 70%
    - Last Bastion (4 def, 8 HP): cant be attacked while another standing tile shares its lane
        - still blocks the lane, so tiles behind it stay shielded
        - if nothing else can be attacked it gets attacked anyway
    - Mercenary Camp (1 def, 4 HP): two passives
        - gold income: +1 gold at the end of each turn
        - mercenary hire: spend 3 gold during the assign phase to hire a random unit (locked ones included)
    - Granary (2 def, 5 HP): if it survives the turn, the card pick shows 4 options instead of 3
    - Militia Center (2 def, 4 HP): at the start of the defense adds 1 Militia to the garrison for each standing House
        - recruits dont count against the garrison picks
    - Infirmary (1 def, 4 HP): while standing all garrison heals 1 HP at the end of each turn
    - unique tiles (Infirmary, Militia Center, Chapel, Last Bastion, Mercenary Camp, Granary) can only be unlocked once, max 1 in the palette
        - once unlocked (or picked earlier in the same city) they stop being offered
- unlockable units: Crossbowmen, Paladins, Longbowmen, Wardens, Captain, Barbarian Berserkers, Skirmishers, Warlord, Standard Bearer
- unit passives:
    - Archers (2 str, 2 HP, 2 end) - the attack they defend is 1 weaker
    - Veterans (3 str, 4 HP, 3 end) - regain +1 endurance after fighting
    - Spearmen (3 str, 3 HP, 3 end) - Spear Wall: +1 strength for each other Spearmen on the same attack
    - Crossbowmen (3 str, 2 HP, 2 end) - the attack they defend is 2 weaker
    - Longbowmen (2 str, 3 HP, 3 end) - the attack they defend is 1 weaker
    - Wardens (3 str, 5 HP, 2 end) - regain +1 endurance after fighting
    - Militia, Knights and Paladins have no passive
    - Captain (2 str, 4 HP, 3 end) - Inspiring: events on its attack are 15% more likely to be good, stacks
    - Barbarian Berserkers (3 str, 4 HP, 1 end) - Last Stand: +2 strength while at 1 HP
    - Sellswords (4 str, 3 HP, 2 end) - Paid Blade:
        - free pick, doesnt use a garrison pick slot
        - can only be picked with at least 1 gold (so never in the first city)
        - costs 1 gold at the end of each turn (after income), leaves when the treasury cant pay
        - can also come from mercenary hire, recruit cards and Wandering Sellswords, same upkeep
    - Skirmishers (2 str, 3 HP, 3 end) - Flanker: +2 strength when the defended tile is exposed from more than one side
    - Warlord (4 str, 5 HP, 2 end) - Bloodlust: +1 strength for each unit that died in this city
    - Standard Bearer (1 str, 4 HP, 3 end) - Banner: +1 strength to every other unit on the same attack, stacks
- Gold:
    - campaign-wide treasury, starts at 0 and carries over between cities
    - earned from tile passives (Mercenary Camp) and the Tribute card
    - spent on mercenaries for now, meant as a general currency for future uses (shops, upgrades, ...)
- end of campaign: turns survived per city, total turns and permanent points
    - permanent points = total turns, not spendable yet
